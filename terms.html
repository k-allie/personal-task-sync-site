Terms of Service

Effective October 19, 2026

Personal Task Sync is a personal automation utility for synchronizing Google Tasks and Apple Reminders on a Mac.

Authorized use

Use the utility only with accounts and task records you are authorized to access. Google authorization and macOS Reminders permission are required. Google and Apple services remain subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Availability

Synchronization requires the Mac to be awake, online, and signed in, with valid authorization. Availability depends on the Mac and connected providers. Continuous availability, instant synchronization, and support for every feature of either task application are not promised.

Supported data and behavior

The utility transfers supported shared fields, including titles, notes, due dates, and completion. Recurrence rules, alerts, attachments, subtask relationships, and other features are not fully transferred. Conflicts can be resolved automatically, and confirmed deletions can propagate when enabled. Keep suitable backups of important task records and review configuration before use.

Personal operation

The utility is provided as a personal-use tool without a service-level agreement. This website supplies information and does not run a hosted synchronization service. Personal Task Sync is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Apple.

Stopping use

You can stop using the utility at any time by disabling its local login service and revoking its permissions. See the Privacy Policy for how to remove locally retained records.

Contact

For questions, use the developer support email displayed on the application's Google authorization screen.
